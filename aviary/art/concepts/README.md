## Condor

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production concept sheet for an original Minecraft Java server ride called Oak Aviary. Create a carefully constructed landscape concept sheet showing ONE consistent friendly giant condor mount, in three views: dominant three-quarter landed view on left with small blocky adult Minecraft rider seated on a simple brown leather saddle, top-down wings spread on upper right, strict side profile with wings folded on lower right. Minecraft-native cuboid geometry, clearly separate rigid cuboid wing bones and broad stepped primary feathers, flat pixel art texture patches, no smooth organic meshes, no rounded cylinders. Wingspan about 7 blocks, body about 2.5 blocks long. Warm ivory head and neck ruff, charcoal brown body, warm chestnut wing coverts, pale gold hooked block beak, amber eyes under a gentle brow, strong brown feet. Readable friendly silhouette, credible seated rider clearance ahead of wing roots, small leather saddle secured around torso, no reins across wings. Restrained original adventure-game charm, precise clean shapes, neutral warm parchment background, soft studio lighting, no photoreal feathers, no elaborate ornamental armor, no logos, no watermark. Only concise English labels: 'CONDOR', 'LANDING', 'WING PLAN', 'FOLDED'. Show all limbs and feathers fully in frame, consistent proportions between views. This must be feasible to rebuild as rigid cube parts and a small number of animated item-display entities in unmodified Minecraft.

Evaluation: the warm head, broad wings and simple saddle give a readable, friendly silhouette. The illustrated feather count was reduced to eight animated rigid parts and 94 cuboids. The Blender model and the vanilla resource pack use the same authored geometry. Wing tips inherit upper-wing motion; the rider remains a native player entity, preserving skin and equipment.

## Aviport

Use case: stylized-concept. Original Minecraft Java game asset production concept: a compact forest AVI PORT landing platform for a friendly giant condor with a seven-block wingspan. Three clear views on one landscape sheet: dominant isometric 3D view, small top-down block construction plan, small side silhouette showing a clear bird approach corridor. Buildable entirely from ordinary Minecraft oak planks, stripped oak logs, stone bricks, fences, lanterns and a bell. Nine by nine block landing deck, front and sky completely open, restrained modest rear signpost saying 'AVI PORT', a simple bell post at rear corner, a two-block-wide stair on the rear side. No roof over landing zone, no tall obstruction in front of platform, enough open room for wings, no buildings, no people needed. Charming woodland adventure art direction, visible block grid, restrained moss and small leaf patches along exterior stone foundation only, readable functional layout, soft natural morning light, warm neutral background. Neat compact diagram labels in English only: 'AVI PORT', 'CLEAR APPROACH', 'LANDING DECK'. Avoid ornate airport terminal, intricate fantasy architecture, excessive foliage, floating stairs, wings intersecting buildings, excessive lettering. This is a practical Minecraft structure blueprint, not photorealism.

Evaluation: the generated front fence conflicts with the required clear approach. The Blender construction study removes that fence, keeps rails on the sides, and places the bell at the rear. The game checks landing clearance rather than blindly reproducing the image. No generated structure is placed automatically in a player's world.
